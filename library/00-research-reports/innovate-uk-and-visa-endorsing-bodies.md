## 1. INNOVATE UK — zero competitive threat, and the flagship runs the wrong way

**GBIP (Global Business Innovation Programme)** — https://iuk-business-connect.org.uk/programme/global-business-innovation/
Verbatim eligibility: **"you must be a small or medium sized company based in the UK"**. It is **outbound** — it takes UK SMEs *to* overseas markets (India is one of them). An Indian company cannot use it to enter the UK. Not a competitor; the mirror image of one.

**Innovate UK Business Growth** (the ex-EEN advisory service) — https://www.innovateukbusinessgrowth.ukri.org/
Free — "our advisory support is funded by the UK Government" — but the site currently states: **"New requests into the service, outside of the above, have been paused as part of the current planning cycle."** Effectively closed to new clients in 2026. UK-based innovative businesses only. Subsidy framing: 100% funded up to £315,000 (Minimal Financial Assistance limit).

**Global Expert Missions** — https://iuk-business-connect.org.uk/programme/global-expert-missions/ — NOT FOUND: no published cost or eligibility criteria. UK-innovator scoping/delegation activity, not an inbound service.

**KTN is now Innovate UK Business Connect** (iuk-business-connect.org.uk). Note its `/sitemap.xml` and `/sitemap_index.xml` return the HTML shell, not XML.

**Answer to the crux question: YES, all Innovate UK routes require the company to be UK-registered/UK-based.** An Indian company must incorporate first.

## 2. BRITISH BUSINESS BANK — near-total exclusion of Indian corporates

Site is Cloudflare-blocked to curl and WebFetch; everything below is from Wayback snapshots (May–June 2026, pages showing © 2026).

**BBB is not a lender**: "We design, deliver, and manage a range of products through over 200 delivery partners." You cannot apply to BBB directly.

**Start Up Loans** — £500–£25,000, fixed **6% p.a.**, 1–5 years, unsecured, personal liability, plus free mentoring. Eligibility verbatim: **"You're a current UK resident"**; **"Your business is based in the UK"**; **"You have the right to work in the UK."** A non-UK-resident Indian director is excluded outright.

**Growth Guarantee Scheme** — facilities up to £2m (£1m where the Northern Ireland Protocol applies); **70% guarantee to the LENDER, not the borrower** — the borrower stays 100% liable. Eligibility verbatim: *"Being engaged in trading activity … in the UK with the core of its business operations in the UK; Generating more than 50% of turnover from trading activity…; **Having a turnover not exceeding £45m per annum (on a group basis, where you are part of a group)**; Having a borrowing proposal considered viable by the lender. Not being a business in difficulty…"* The group test has **no geographic limit**, so the Indian parent's global turnover counts — most Indian corporates are out. Also: "Banks, building societies, insurance companies, public-sector bodies, and state-funded primary and secondary schools are not eligible."

**ENABLE (Guarantees / Build / Funding / Community ENABLE Funding)** — purely **wholesale**: "We support lenders such as banks and non-bank financial institutions unlock more lending to smaller businesses." ENABLE Guarantees is "open to all UK banks, UK branches of foreign banks, asset and asset-based finance providers…". A business cannot access ENABLE. Zero relevance.

**Nations and Regions Investment Funds** (all "open for applications", deployed via appointed regional fund managers): Northern Powerhouse Investment Fund II **£660m**; Midlands Engine Investment Fund II **£400m**; South West Investment Fund **£200m**; Investment Fund for Scotland **£150m**; Investment Fund for Wales **£130m**; Investment Fund for Northern Ireland **~£100m**; East of England and South East Investment Funds "coming soon". Typical structure: Smaller Loans **£25k–£100k**, Debt Finance **£100k–£2m**, Equity **up to £5m**. **The binding constraint is geographic, not ownership** — London and most of the South East are not covered, which is where Indian corporates actually land. NOT FOUND: any explicit foreign-ownership exclusion; fund managers apply UK subsidy-control and SME tests, and an Indian group would normally fail the SME test.

## 3. VISA ROUTES + ENDORSING BODIES

### Global Talent — CORRECTION: Tech Nation has not collapsed
Verbatim, https://www.gov.uk/global-talent-digital-technology (live today): **"The Home Office will send your application to be reviewed by Tech Nation, a UK organisation ('endorsing body') with expertise in your field."** Decision "within 5 to 8 weeks". Tech Nation charges **nothing** (technation.io: "The review process is free").
Fees are Home Office only: **£561 endorsement + £205 visa = £766**; dependants £766 each; IHS £1,035/yr/person. Other competent bodies: Royal Society, British Academy, Royal Academy of Engineering, Arts Council England — same split.
**Requires no UK entity, no UK address, no sponsor, no job offer.** This route entirely bypasses the boutique's product.

### Innovator Founder — four endorsing bodies, all ~£1,000
Home Office statutory caps: "Endorsement fee under the Innovator Founder route (payable to endorsing body)" **£1,000 excl VAT**; "Contact point meeting" **£500 excl VAT**.
- **UK Endorsing Services** (https://www.ukesapp.co.uk/terms-and-conditions): New Business / Same Business / Settlement "£1,000 (excl. VAT)" each; 12-month checkpoint **£500 excl VAT** (~10 months after business start date); 24-month checkpoint **£500 excl VAT** (~22 months); "Scale up assessment fee: £1,500 (excl. VAT)"; cancellation before assessment refunded "minus a 10% administrative fee"; nothing refunded once work commenced or if refused; late payment interest 8% over base.
- **Innovator International** (https://www.innovatorinternational.com/innovator-founder-visa-uk-endorsing-body/innovator/): "The fee for your assessment is £1k (+vat where applicable). If successful, you will also be required to pay a monitoring fee of £1k (+vat) after 12 months, and a final assessment fee of £1k (+ vat) should you choose to apply for ILR or another (same) Innovator Visa. There are no other mandatory costs." Initial £1k non-refundable.
- **Envestors** (https://envestors-visa-endorsement.co.uk/faq): "Fees for the programme are set by the Home Office. All mandatory fees are subject to Vat once the applicant resides in the UK." Re-application needs a fresh £1,000.
- **Global Entrepreneur Programme** — free, invite-only, gives a one-time IF endorsement.
Lifetime endorsement spend ≈ **£3,000 excl VAT** plus visa fees. Visa: **£1,357** outside UK / **£1,693** in-UK switch.

## 3b. THE CRUX — which routes require a UK entity, UK address, or UK-based Authorising Officer

**Tier A — nothing UK-side required at application:**
- **Global Talent**: no entity, no address, no sponsor, no job offer.
- **Innovator Founder at first application**: Appendix Innovator Founder INNF 8.1–8.3 imposes **no Companies House requirement** for a new-business endorsement.

**Tier B — UK entity deferred but unavoidable:**
- **Innovator Founder at extension/settlement**. Verbatim INNF 9.5: **"The applicant's business must be registered with Companies House and the applicant must be listed as a director or member of that business."** Same at settlement. Incorporation is postponed ~3 years, not avoided; endorsing-body checkpoints at 12 and 24 months police progress.

**Tier C — hard UK footprint and UK-based people (all sponsored routes).** Sponsor guidance Part 1, verbatim:
- **L8.3**: "If you have no operating or trading presence in the UK (or, in the case of UK Expansion Worker, if you have no UK footprint), we will refuse your application."
- **L4.9**: Key Personnel must "be based in the UK for the period they will fill the role (there is an exception for the UK Expansion Worker route)".
- **L4.33**: "Your Authorising Officer must be a paid member of staff or an office holder within your organisation. **You cannot appoint a representative or somebody from another organisation to fill this role.**"
- **L4.18**: a UK-based representative "cannot be your Authorising Officer."
- **L4.20**: "The representative must be based in the UK – you cannot appoint an overseas-based representative for any Key Personnel role."
- **L6.7**: representatives "must be based in the UK".
- **L8.9** gives an explicit anti-shell-company refusal example; **L8.20** states that virtual/serviced-office business models trigger a compliance visit to the Authorising Officer's physical address.

**Commercial conclusion:** a nominee director **cannot** be sold as the Authorising Officer — the AO must be a paid employee or office holder of the sponsor and (except UKEW) UK-based. But a UK registered address **is** mandatory and **is** inspectable, so the registered-office/premises product is squarely in scope. The one thing that cannot be outsourced is a real UK-based senior person.

**UK Expansion Worker** (Sponsor a Global Business Mobility Worker, version 04/26, valid from 8 April 2026), verbatim:
- **GBM3.3**: "you must not already be actively trading in the UK".
- **GBM3.4**: "You must, however, show you have a UK 'footprint'. You can show this by providing evidence you have either: UK business premises – for example, a lease agreement or documents showing you have purchased the premises; registered your UK business with Companies House as either an overseas company branch or a new company that is a subsidiary of the overseas linked business – in this case, you need only provide your Companies House reference number".
- GBM3.5: no footprint = rejection/refusal. GBM3.6: overseas business active and trading ≥3 years (Japan/Australia FTA and certain stock-exchange-listing exemptions). GBM3.8: investment-only vehicles ineligible. GBM3.13: expansion must be the same type of business, wholly owned / branch / sister company. GBM3.21–3.25: AO may be overseas **only** if they personally assign their own CoS, in which case they must also be the Level 1 User. GBM3.26: CoS capped at 10 (capped at **1** if the AO is overseas). GBM3.29: **Provisional** (not A) rating if the AO is overseas. GBM3.37: licence valid 4 years, non-renewable. GBM3.38: must establish full UK trading presence within 2 years or CoS allocation drops to zero.
- Salary: **£52,500** or the going rate, whichever is higher; "high earner" = **£73,900** (exempts the 12-month prior-employment rule). Max 2 years, no settlement.

**This is the boutique's strongest product-market fit.** Companies House incorporation *or* a lease is the literal entry ticket to UKEW; no free programme supplies either, and the guidance explicitly flags incorporation as the low-friction option ("you need only provide your Companies House reference number").

**Scale-up Worker** (https://www.gov.uk/scale-up-worker-visa) requires an already-trading, already-audited UK entity. Verbatim: "To be an eligible fast-growing business, in the 3 year period immediately before they were approved by the Home Office to sponsor scale-up workers, they must have: grown by 20% on average in each year in either employment or total sales (turnover); had at least 10 employees at the start of the 3 year period." Salary floor £39,100 or the going rate. Endorsing Body Pathway endorsement fee **£1,500 excl VAT**. Irrelevant to a fresh Indian entrant.

Free, no equity ("The GEP is a UK government programme and does not take equity in participating companies and does not charge for participation"), inbound-only ("Not currently based in the UK"), invite-only, and is an Innovator Founder endorsing body offering a one-time endorsement. https://www.business.gov.uk/campaign/gep/frequently-asked-questions/

## 5. THE REAL COMPETITIVE THREAT — free DBT support (bigger than Innovate UK and BBB combined)

- **Free "Expand your business in the UK" guide** — https://www.business.gov.uk/invest-in-uk/expand-your-business-in-the-uk/guide/ — with free sub-guides covering exactly the boutique's advisory layer: "Set up and register your business" (branch vs subsidiary vs JV with pros/cons and timelines: "It typically takes one month to set up a branch"; a subsidiary "typically takes one day"), "How to apply for a visa", "Find the right location and premises", "How to choose and set up a UK bank account", "How to register for tax and claim tax allowances", "Know UK employment regulations". Plus sector-specific versions (Healthcare & Life Sciences, Financial/Professional & Business Services, Food & Drink, Consumer & Retail).
- **DBT Investment Support Directory** — https://www.business.gov.uk/international/investment-support-directory/find-a-specialist/ — a free government matchmaking directory of UK advisers, filterable by service. Counts pulled today: **Company incorporation 21 firms; Immigration 19; Accounting and tax 18; Mergers and acquisitions 14.** Listed names include ABBISS CADRES LLP, BURGES SALMON, GOODWILLE LTD ("essential business services to overseas companies looking to enter, grow or scale in the UK"), GENTIUM INTERNATIONAL, KEYSWOT. Two implications: (a) this is the pre-curated competitor set handed free to every inbound Indian corporate; (b) it is also a free acquisition channel the boutique should list on. The bundle's four components map onto four existing filters, so it is undifferentiated on the directory's face.
- **OfI fast-track sponsor licence referral** — https://www.business.gov.uk/campaign/global-talent/global-talent-support-for-businesses/ofi-fast-track-referral-for-a-uk-expansion-worker-sponsor-licence/ — 10 working days vs 8 weeks; globaltalenthub@officeforinvestment.gov.uk. Gated to firms already receiving OfI support, in one of the 8 Industrial Strategy sectors, and meeting one of: "£1 million venture capital or institutional investment" received / "£2 million capital investment for UK expansion" committed / membership of a recognised high-growth programme. For everyone who does not qualify, the paid substitute is the **£750** Home Office priority service.
- **Visa fees reimbursement scheme for scale-ups** — https://www.business.gov.uk/campaign/global-talent/global-talent-support-for-businesses/visa-fees-reimbursement-scheme-for-scale-ups/ (updated 9 June 2026). Clean Energy / Life Sciences / Digital & Technology. Must "Hold a valid UK Sponsor Licence", "Have already established a presence in the UK", have a UK bank account, pass DBT due diligence. Covers Skilled Worker, Global Talent, Scale-Up. Eligibility is circular — you must already have bought what the boutique sells.
