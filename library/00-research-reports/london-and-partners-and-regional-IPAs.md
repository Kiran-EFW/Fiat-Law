# COMMERCIAL BOTTOM LINE

1. **What's free, and it's a lot:** sector intelligence, site/property search, hosted delegation visits, talent and salary analysis, introductions to councils, universities, banks, recruiters and professional firms, grant navigation, named account managers. Every agency gives all of this away. Don't try to sell any of it.

2. **What is uniformly absent — your gap.** Not one agency in Part A or Part B offers: giving legal advice, incorporation as a service, nominee director, registered office, bookkeeping/statutory accounts, or M&A advisory. They all stop at the introduction and disclaim liability. SDI literally publishes a page telling you to find a solicitor via the Law Society. Wales publishes a directory of third-party providers and says *"Listing should not be taken as endorsement of any kind."* L&P's T&Cs say services are *"provided by our partners... and not L&P"* and *"L&P accepts no liability."*

3. **WTP compression is on the advisory/orientation layer only.** You cannot charge for market intelligence, intros or a site visit; you can charge fully for signature, liability and regulated delivery. The disclaimers *are* your pricing justification — put L&P 2.3 (*"Any support provided to you is for information purposes only"*), SDI (*"does not constitute advice... seek the advice of an appropriately qualified person"*), and Enterprise Oxfordshire (*"We cannot confirm that they are legally entitled or professionally qualified"*) on one slide.

4. **Channel, not competitor.** L&P (Grow London Member/Growth Partner), WMGC (Partners West Midlands), Invest Manchester (£4k/£8k/£16k) and Wales (Soft Landing Directory) all sell listings/partner status. Getting into these directories is how the free agencies become your top of funnel. Redfern Legal, Oury Clark, IMA and GoGlobal are already in L&P's directory doing exactly your bundle.

5. **India-specific ranking of who will be in the room with your prospects:** (1) **Invest NI** — Mumbai office + named India staff + explicit "soft landing support" language; (2) **London & Partners** — Mumbai BKC and Bengaluru; (3) **SDI** — Mumbai office; (4) **Invest Manchester** — Manchester India Partnership; (5) **WMGC** — mayoral missions only; (6) **West Yorkshire** — missions only, no service; (7) Bristol, Oxfordshire, Kent, Cambridge — nothing.

6. **Honest gaps.** L&P GLA grant £ figure NOT PUBLISHED in reachable pages. L&P Strategic/Growth Partner fees NOT PUBLISHED. WMGC PWM fees NOT PUBLISHED (they say the team will "talk you through the different tiers"). Invest NI has no published disclaimer at all (both `/disclaimer` and `/terms-and-conditions` 404). Invest NI job-creation/investment minimums NOT PUBLISHED. No L&P product called "Business Growth Programme" exists.
