Dear Minister Goyal, 
In connection with the signing ofthe Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement between 
the Government of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and the 
Government of the Republic of India (“the Agreement”), | have the honour to confirm the 
following understanding reached between the Government of the United Kingdom of Great 
Britain and Northern Ireland (‘the United Kingdom”) and the Government of the Republic of 
India (“India”) during the course of the negotiation of the Agreement: 
1. India takes note of the fact that the Bailiwicks of Guernsey and Jersey and the Isle of 
Man are territories for whose international relations the United Kingdom is responsible, 
and the United Kingdom’s intention to extend the provisions of the Agreement relating 
to trade in services to those territories. 
The United Kingdom and India will enter into discussions with a view to considering 
the extension of the provisions of the Agreement relating to trade in services to the 
Bailiwicks of Guernsey and Jersey and the Isle of Man as soon as practicable for the 
United Kingdom and India. 
If the United Kingdom and India agree to the extension of the provisions of the 
Agreement relating to trade in services to the Bailiwicks of Guernsey and J 
the Isle of Man, any amendments to the Agreement required to accommodate any such 
extension will be made in accordance with Article 30.3 (Territorial Extension Final
— 
Provisions) of the Agreement and come into effect after entry into force of the 
Agreement. 
look forward to your letter in reply confirming that your Government shares this 
understanding.
