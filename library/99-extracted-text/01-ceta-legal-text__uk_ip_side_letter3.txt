Dear Minister Goyal, 
In connection with the signing on this date of the Comprehensive Economic and Trade 
Agreement between the Government of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern 
Ireland and the Government of the Republic of India ("the Agreement"), I have the honour to 
confinn the following agreement reached between the Government of the United Kingdom of 
Great Britain and Northern Ireland (''the United Kingdom") and the Government of the 
Republic of India ("India") (together ''the Parties", severally "a Party") during the course of 
the negotiations of the Agreement with regards to Article 13.69 (Tenn of Protection -
Intellectual Property Rights): 
l. The Parties shall, following entry into force of the Agreement, enter into consultations 
to review Article 13.69 if, during the period commencing on the date the Agreement is 
signed by the Parties and ending on the date of entry into force of the Agreement, an 
international agreement enters into force between India and a non-Party that includes 
obligations to increase their term of protection provided under Article 13.69. 
2. If the event described in paragraph I occurs, the Parties shall: 
(a) enter into consultations under the auspices ofthe Working Group on Intellectual 
Property Rights established pursuant to Article 13.15 (Working Group on 
Intellectual Property Rights - Intellectual Property Rights) of the Agreement as 
soon as reasonably practicable after the date ofentry into force ofthe Agreement 
and, in any event, no later than one year after the date of entry into force of the 
Agreement; 
(b) where these consultations are entered into, review Article 13.69 with a view to 
increasing the term ofprotection following the event referred to under paragraph 
1; and 
(c) following these consultations, complete the review under this Side Letter in a 
timely manner. 
3. This Side Letter shall constitute an integral part of the Agreement. 
I have the honour to propose that this letter and your letter of confinnation in reply shall 
constitute an agreement between the United Kingdom and India, which shall enter into force 
on the date on which the Agreement enters into force for both the United Kingdom and India. 
cerely, ~ 
THE RT REYNOLDS MP 
Secretary of State for Business and Trade and President of the Board of Trade
