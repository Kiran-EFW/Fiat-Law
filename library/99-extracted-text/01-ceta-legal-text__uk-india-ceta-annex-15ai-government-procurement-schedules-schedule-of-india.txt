Annex 15A - INDIA - 5 
1. Chapter 15 (Government Procurement) only covers those central government 
entities listed in this Section and any subordinate entity of those central 
government entities conducting procurement in an independent manner. 1 Each of 
such subordinate entities shall be treated as a separate procuring entity for the 
purposes of Chapter 15 (Government Procurement) and this Section. 
2. All central public sector enterprises (“ CPSEs”) established or maintained by the 
central government entities listed in this Section are covered, 2 except any CPSE 
exposed to competitive market forces in any of its operations. The determination 
of exposure to competitive market forces shall be based on the following factors: 
(a) there is an actual or potential presence of one or more persons providing 
similar or equivalent goods or services in the market the CPSE operates in; 
(b) the price of goods or services offered by the CPSE is determined on a 
market basis; or 
(c) there is no restriction on access to the market the CPSE operates in under 
any laws, rules or regulations. 
An indicative list of CPSEs determined not to be exposed to competitive market 
forces is provided below: 
i. Food Corporation of India  
ii. National Research Development Corporation  
iii. Uranium Corporation of India

Annex 15A - INDIA - 12 
Section G 
General Notes 
1. Chapter 15 (Government Procurement) shall not apply to: 
(a) procurements for the protection of essential security interests including the 
procurement of arms, ammunition or war materials, or procurements 
indispensable for national security or for national defence purposes; 
(b) preferences provided under the Public Procurement Policy for Micro and 
Small Enterprises (MSEs) Order, 2012, as amended from time to time; 
(c) procurements of agricultural products made in furtherance of agricultural 
support programmes and human feeding programmes (e.g. food aid 
including urgent relief aid); 
(d) procurements of goods or services for the establishment and operation of 
any overseas mission or subordinate or attached office or autonomous or 
other entity of any nature outside the territory of India; 
(e) procurements by a procuring entity from another government entity; 
(f) procurements of goods or services outside the territory of India for 
consumption outside the territory of India; or 
(g) procurements by any entity not covered by Chapter 15 Government 
Procurement or Section A (Central Government Entities). 
 
  
Annex 15A - INDIA - 13 
Section H 
Applicable Threshold Adjustment Formula 
1. India shall calculate and publish the value of the thresholds under Chapter 15 
(Government Procurement) expressed in Indian Rupee after the Parties have 
exchanged written notifications confirming that they have completed their 
respective domestic legal requirements, necessary for entry into force of this 
Agreement and before the date of entry into force of this Agreement. 
2. India shall notify the United Kingdom, not later than two years from the date of 
previous notification, of the then current thresholds in its currency and the method 
of calculating such thresholds. India shall calculate and convert the value of the 
threshold under Chapter 15 (Government Procurement) expressed in Indian Rupee. 
3. These calculations shall be based on the conversion rates published by the 
International Monetary Fund. 
4. India shall consult if a major change in its national currency relative to the SDR or 
to the national currency of India were to create a significant problem with regard 
to the application of Chapter 15 (Government Procurement).
